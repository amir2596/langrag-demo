# Getting Started with Nimbus

Nimbus is a project management tool for small teams. This guide covers
account setup and your first project.

## Creating an account

Sign up at nimbus.example.com with a work email. Free accounts include
up to 3 projects and 5 team members. Paid plans (see the billing FAQ)
remove these limits.

## Creating your first project

1. Click "New Project" in the top-left sidebar.
2. Choose a template: Kanban board, list view, or calendar view. You
   can switch views later without losing data.
3. Invite teammates by email from the Members tab. Invited members
   must accept within 14 days or the invite expires.

## Key concepts

- A **Workspace** contains multiple projects and is shared by your
  whole team.
- A **Board** is one project's view of its tasks.
- **Automations** are if-this-then-that rules scoped to a single
  board (e.g. "when a task moves to Done, notify the assignee").
  Nimbus supports up to 10 active automations per board on the Team
  plan, and unlimited automations on the Business plan.

## Mobile app

The Nimbus mobile app (iOS and Android) supports viewing and
commenting on tasks, but automation editing is desktop-only as of the
current release.
