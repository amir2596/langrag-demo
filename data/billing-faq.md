# Nimbus Billing FAQ

## Plans and pricing

- **Free**: $0/month. Up to 3 projects, 5 members, 10 automations per
  board.
- **Team**: $9/user/month, billed annually ($12/user month-to-month).
  Unlimited projects, up to 50 members, 10 automations per board.
- **Business**: $19/user/month, billed annually. Unlimited members,
  unlimited automations, SSO, and priority support with a 4-hour
  first-response SLA.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the current
billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next
billing cycle — you keep full access to your current plan's features
until then.

## Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel anytime from Settings > Billing. Annual plans are
refundable within 30 days of purchase; monthly plans are not
refundable for partial months. Cancelling does not delete your data —
workspaces are kept for 90 days in a read-only state before deletion,
so you can reactivate within that window without losing anything.

## Payment methods

Nimbus accepts major credit cards and, for Business plan annual
contracts only, bank transfer / invoicing. Payment is processed in
USD regardless of your account's region.
